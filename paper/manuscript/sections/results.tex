% \paragraph{RQ1：为什么 RS 改善了 rationale，却未改善评分？}
\paragraph{RQ1: Why Does RS Improve Rationales but Not Scoring?}

% Figure~\ref{fig:training-targets-by-task-type} 比较了 SO 与 RS 在
% 两类任务上的评分性能。在四个序数任务上（左），SO 在 DS 和 RF
% 下的平均 QWK 均高于 RS（0.747 对 0.696；0.712 对 0.679）。
% 在三个二分类任务上（右），SO 在 DS 下的优势较大（0.928 对
% 0.843），但在 RF 下两者几乎持平（0.923 对 0.922）。因此，
% SO 总体上具有更强的评分性能，而 RS 仅在二分类任务的 RF 接口下
% 接近 SO。完整的逐任务结果见 Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}。

Figure~\ref{fig:training-targets-by-task-type} compares the scoring
performance of SO and RS across the two task types. On the four ordinal
tasks (left), SO achieves a higher mean QWK than RS under both DS and RF
(0.747 versus 0.696 and 0.712 versus 0.679, respectively). On the three
binary tasks (right), SO has a larger advantage under DS (0.928 versus
0.843), whereas the two training targets are nearly tied under RF
(0.923 versus 0.922). Thus, SO provides stronger scoring performance
overall, while RS approaches SO only on binary tasks under RF. Full
task-level results are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}.


% \paragraph{RS 改善理由拟合与质量，但未改善评分。}
% 表~\ref{tab:rationale-summary} 汇总了 Qwen3-4B 的 rationale 诊断结果。
% 与 SO 相比，RS 在每个任务上都取得更低的 teacher-forced rationale NLL、
% 更高的平均 rationale 质量，以及更强的 rationale--score 一致性。
% 在配对比较中，350 例里 RS rationale 获得 248 次偏好，SO rationale
% 获得 25 次，其余 77 组被判断为相当。尽管具有这些优势，RS 在 DS 和
% RF 推理下的序数 QWK 均低于 SO。因此，更好的 rationale 拟合以及
% 对模型自身预测的更强支持，并不必然意味着评分更准确。完整 NLL 诊断和
% rationale 评测结果分别见表~\ref{tab:rationale-nll-full} 和
% 表~\ref{tab:rationale-quality-full}。

\paragraph{RS improves rationale fit and quality without improving scoring.}
Table~\ref{tab:rationale-summary} summarizes the rationale diagnostics
for Qwen3-4B.
Compared with SO, RS achieves lower teacher-forced rationale NLL
on every task, higher mean rationale quality,
and stronger rationale--score consistency.
In the paired comparison, RS rationales are preferred in 248 of
350 cases, SO rationales in 25 cases, and the remaining 77 pairs
are judged comparable.
Despite these advantages, RS has lower ordinal QWK than SO
under both DS and RF inference.
Thus, better rationale fitting and stronger support for the
model's own prediction do not necessarily imply more accurate scores.
Full NLL diagnostics and rationale-evaluation results are provided
in Tables~\ref{tab:rationale-nll-full}
and~\ref{tab:rationale-quality-full}, respectively.

% \paragraph{RQ2：RF 推理如何影响评分性能？}
\paragraph{RQ2: How Does RF Inference Affect Scoring Performance?}

% 我们分析 Qwen3-4B 从 DS 切换至 RF 后的评分表现变化。
% 表~\ref{tab:interface-changes-by-task-type} 分别汇总序数与二分类任务的变化。
% 在四个序数任务上，SO 和 RS 的 QWK 分别下降
% 0.035 和 0.017；RS 的准确率虽提高 3.49 个百分点，但严重错误率
% 同时增加 4.26 个百分点，抵消了精确预测增加带来的收益。在三个
% 二分类任务上，RF 使 SO 的 Macro-F1 小幅下降 0.006，却使 RS
% 提高 0.079，并将其错误率和格式无效率分别降低 5.59 和
% 3.45 个百分点。因此，SO 在两类任务上均更适合 DS，而 RS 仅在
% 二分类任务上受益于 RF。

We analyze how switching from DS to RF affects scoring performance on
Qwen3-4B. Table~\ref{tab:interface-changes-by-task-type} summarizes the
changes separately for ordinal and binary tasks. On the four ordinal tasks,
QWK decreases
by 0.035 under SO and 0.017 under RS. Although RS gains 3.49 percentage
points in accuracy, its severe-error rate also rises by 4.26 points,
offsetting the increase in exact predictions. On the three binary tasks,
RF slightly reduces Macro-F1 under SO by 0.006 but increases it under RS
by 0.079, while reducing the RS incorrect-output and format-invalid rates
by 5.59 and 3.45 points, respectively. Thus, SO favors DS across both
task types, whereas RS benefits from RF only on binary tasks.

% \textbf{RF 推理可能在早期形成错误判断，并将其延续至最终评分。}
% Table~\ref{tab:full-interface-transitions} 显示，当推理协议从 DS
% 切换至 RF 时，在 SO 和 RS 两种监督目标下，有害预测转移均多于
% 有益预测转移，但 RS 下两者的差距较小。在完成共识判定的
% 1,119 个所选转移中，701 个有害转移中有 700 个
% 的完整 RF rationale 支持错误的 RF 分数，而 418 个有益转移的
% rationale 均支持 gold score。Figure~\ref{fig:rationale-error-analysis}
% 进一步显示，评分标准误用和分数映射错误是最常见的错误类型，且
% 74.9\% 的首个错误出现在 rationale 的前半部分。综合来看，这些
% 结果表明，RF 推理会影响最终评分，其影响更多表现为有害转移，
% 且相关错误通常在 rationale 的早期阶段出现。完整统计和裁判协议
% 见 Appendix~\ref{app:sentence-level-analysis}。

\textbf{RF inference can form erroneous judgments early and carry them
into the final score.}
Table~\ref{tab:full-interface-transitions} shows that, when the inference
protocol switches from DS to RF, harmful prediction transitions
outnumber beneficial ones under both supervision targets, SO and RS,
although the gap is smaller under RS. Among the 1,119 selected transitions
with completed consensus judgments, the complete RF rationale supported
the incorrect RF score in 700 of the 701 harmful transitions, whereas the
rationales for all 418 beneficial transitions supported the gold score.
Figure~\ref{fig:rationale-error-analysis}
further shows that rubric misapplication and score-mapping errors are
the most common error types, with 74.9\% of first flagged sentences occurring in
the first half of the rationale; unresolved error-type ties count as flags in
this position statistic. Taken together, these results suggest
that RF inference affects the final score, with its effects more often
manifesting as harmful transitions and the associated flags typically
emerging early in the rationale. Full statistics and the adjudication
protocol are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:sentence-level-analysis}.

\textbf{Score recovery after RF rationale correction provides
intervention evidence that reasoning errors affect final scoring.}
Table~\ref{tab:rationale-correction} compares the three conditions on 158
selected harmful cases.
NatRF and FixOrig both have 0\% accuracy,
with nearly identical MAE (2.038 versus 2.044).
FixOrig exactly reproduces 157 of 158 NatRF predictions
(Appendix~\ref{app:full-results},
Table~\ref{tab:full-correction-aggregate}),
validating the fixed-prefix rescoring control.
FixCorr raises accuracy to 89.87\%
while reducing MAE to 0.101; all remaining 16 predictions improve
from severe to adjacent errors. A post hoc rubric-consistency review flagged
two Actionability edits that treated direct-action questions as implicit.
Excluding these edits, FixCorr still recovers 140 of 156 cases (89.74\%)
with an MAE of 0.103. Per-task recovery rates are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:full-correction-task}.

% \paragraph{RQ3：PaIR 和 RePaIR 在两种接口下表现如何？}
\paragraph{RQ3: How Do PaIR and RePaIR Perform across Interfaces?}

% 表~\ref{tab:model-scale-summary} 比较两个 Qwen3 规模下的五种训练配置，
% 并加入公开的 SciRM-7B 与 SciRM-Ref-7B 基线。PaIR 在 DS 下最优或接近最优，
% RePaIR 在两个规模下均取得最高的 RF 序数 QWK；Qwen3 逐任务结果见
% 表~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level}。

Table~\ref{tab:model-scale-summary} compares five training configurations
on both Qwen3 scales with the released SciRM-7B and SciRM-Ref-7B baselines.
PaIR is strongest or near-strongest under DS, whereas RePaIR has the highest
RF ordinal QWK at both scales; task-level Qwen3 results appear in
Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level}.



% \textbf{ReaSon 改善 RF 序数评分。}
% 在 Qwen3-4B 上，ReaSon 将 RF QWK 从 0.679 提高到 0.707，Macro-F1
% 基本不变（0.922 对 0.921）；DS 仍受格式失败影响
%（Table~\ref{tab:strict-invalid-rates-all-methods}）。

\textbf{ReaSon improves RF ordinal scoring.}
On Qwen3-4B, ReaSon raises RF QWK from 0.679 to 0.707 while leaving
Macro-F1 nearly unchanged (0.922 versus 0.921); DS remains limited by
formatting failures (Table~\ref{tab:strict-invalid-rates-all-methods}).

% \textbf{PaIR 主要改善 DS 评分。}
% 相比 RS，PaIR 将 DS QWK/Macro-F1 从 0.696/0.843 提高到 0.757/0.934。
% 匹配的 PaIR-BP 消融显示，视图级平均在 DS/RF 下分别提高 aggregate QWK
% 0.015/0.008，并提高 Macro-F1 0.010/0.004。

\textbf{PaIR primarily improves DS scoring.}
Relative to RS, PaIR raises DS QWK/Macro-F1 from 0.696/0.843 to
0.757/0.934. The matched PaIR-BP ablation shows that view-level averaging
improves aggregate QWK by 0.015/0.008 and Macro-F1 by 0.010/0.004 under
DS/RF, respectively (Table~\ref{tab:pair-bp-ablation}).


% \textbf{RePaIR 支持两种推理接口。}
% RePaIR 的 DS QWK/Macro-F1 为 0.758/0.931，接近 PaIR 的 0.757/0.934；
% RF QWK 为 0.735，高于 PaIR 的 0.698 和 ReaSon 的 0.707，RF Macro-F1
% 与 PaIR 接近（0.926 对 0.929）。

\textbf{RePaIR supports both inference interfaces.}
RePaIR's DS QWK/Macro-F1 (0.758/0.931) closely matches PaIR
(0.757/0.934). Under RF, its QWK of 0.735 exceeds PaIR's 0.698 and
ReaSon's 0.707, while Macro-F1 remains close to PaIR (0.926 versus 0.929).
