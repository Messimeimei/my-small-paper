Unless otherwise noted, all main results use strict parsing, under which
only scores matching the required output format are considered valid;
format-invalid outputs count as errors for Accuracy and Macro-F1 and are
excluded from QWK, Pearson, and MAE.

% Figure~\ref{fig:training-targets-by-task-type} 比较了 SO 与 RS 在
% 两类任务上的评分性能。在四个序数任务上（左），SO 在 DS 和 RF
% 下的平均 QWK 均高于 RS（0.747 对 0.696；0.712 对 0.679）。
% 在三个二分类任务上（右），SO 在 DS 下的优势较大（0.928 对

% SO 总体上具有更强的评分性能，而 RS 仅在二分类任务的 RF 接口下
% 接近 SO。完整的逐任务结果见 Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}。

Figure~\ref{fig:training-targets-by-task-type} compares the scoring
performance of SO and RS across the two task types. On the four ordinal
tasks (left), SO achieves a higher mean QWK than RS under both DS and RF
(0.747 versus 0.696 and 0.712 versus 0.679, respectively). On the three
binary tasks (right), SO has a larger advantage under DS (0.928 versus
0.843), whereas the two training targets are nearly tied under RF
(0.923 versus 0.922). Thus, SO provides stronger scoring performance
overall, while RS approaches SO only on binary tasks under RF. Full
task-level results are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}.

\textbf{RS fits teacher rationales better without gaining a scoring
advantage.}
Table~\ref{tab:rationale-nll} shows that the mean teacher-forced
rationale NLL decreases from 3.4480 for Base to 1.6964 for SO and
0.7793 for RS, with RS attaining the lowest value on every task.
Together with the weaker scoring performance of RS in
Figure~\ref{fig:training-targets-by-task-type}, these results show that
although RS fits teacher rationales better, this improved fit does not
lead to better scoring performance.

\textbf{RS produces higher-quality rationales.}
Table~\ref{tab:rationale-quality-consistency} shows that RS rationales
were preferred in 248 of the 350 paired comparisons, compared with only
25 preferences for SO; the remaining 77 pairs were comparable. RS also
scored higher on all four quality dimensions, increasing the
four-dimension mean from 2.400 to 2.832 and rationale--score consistency
from 74.3\% to 96.6\%. Thus, the advantage of RS lies in rationale
quality and consistency rather than final scoring performance.

% \paragraph{RQ2：RF 推理为何会降低评分性能？}
\paragraph{RQ2: Why Does RF Inference Degrade Scoring Performance?}

% Table~\ref{tab:interface-changes-by-task-type} 汇总了从 DS 切换至
% RF 后的变化。在四个序数任务上，SO 和 RS 的 QWK 分别下降
% 0.035 和 0.017；RS 的准确率虽提高 3.49 个百分点，但严重错误率
% 同时增加 4.26 个百分点，抵消了精确预测增加带来的收益。在三个
% 二分类任务上，RF 使 SO 的 Macro-F1 小幅下降 0.006，却使 RS
% 提高 0.079，并将其错误率和格式无效率分别降低 5.59 和
% 3.45 个百分点。因此，SO 在两类任务上均更适合 DS，而 RS 仅在
% 二分类任务上受益于 RF。

Table~\ref{tab:interface-changes-by-task-type} summarizes the changes
after switching from DS to RF. On the four ordinal tasks, QWK decreases
by 0.035 under SO and 0.017 under RS. Although RS gains 3.49 percentage
points in accuracy, its severe-error rate also rises by 4.26 points,
offsetting the increase in exact predictions. On the three binary tasks,
RF slightly reduces Macro-F1 under SO by 0.006 but increases it under RS
by 0.079, while reducing the RS incorrect-output and format-invalid rates
by 5.59 and 3.45 points, respectively. Thus, SO favors DS across both
task types, whereas RS benefits from RF only on binary tasks.

% \textbf{RF 推理可能在早期形成错误判断，并将其延续至最终评分。}
% Table~\ref{tab:full-interface-transitions} 显示，当推理协议从 DS
% 切换至 RF 时，在 SO 和 RS 两种监督目标下，有害预测转移均多于
% 有益预测转移，但 RS 下两者的差距较小。在从 DS 切换至 RF 后
% 预测结果发生转移的 1,119 个样本中，701 个有害转移中有 700 个
% 的完整 RF rationale 支持错误的 RF 分数，而 418 个有益转移的
% rationale 均支持 gold score。Figure~\ref{fig:rationale-error-analysis}
% 进一步显示，评分标准误用和分数映射错误是最常见的错误类型，且
% 74.9\% 的首个错误出现在 rationale 的前半部分。综合来看，这些
% 结果表明，RF 推理会影响最终评分，其影响更多表现为有害转移，
% 且相关错误通常在 rationale 的早期阶段出现。完整统计和裁判协议
% 见 Appendix~\ref{app:sentence-level-analysis}。

\textbf{RF inference can form erroneous judgments early and carry them
into the final score.}
Table~\ref{tab:full-interface-transitions} shows that, when the inference
protocol switches from DS to RF, harmful prediction transitions
outnumber beneficial ones under both supervision targets, SO and RS,
although the gap is smaller under RS. Among the 1,119 samples whose
predictions changed after switching from DS to RF, the complete RF
rationale supported the incorrect RF score in 700 of the 701 harmful
transitions, whereas the rationales for all 418 beneficial transitions
supported the gold score. Figure~\ref{fig:rationale-error-analysis}
further shows that rubric misapplication and score-mapping errors are
the most common error types, with 74.9\% of first errors occurring in
the first half of the rationale. Taken together, these results suggest
that RF inference affects the final score, with its effects more often
manifesting as harmful transitions and the associated errors typically
emerging early in the rationale. Full statistics and the adjudication
protocol are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:sentence-level-analysis}.

% \textbf{RF rationale 校正后的评分恢复为推理错误影响最终评分提供了干预证据。}
% FixOrig 在 157/158 个案例中复现了 NatRF 的原错误分数，验证了
% 固定前缀重评分设置的可靠性。在此基础上，FixCorr 进一步使
% 142/158 个案例恢复为 gold score，其余 16 个均由严重错误缩小为
% 相邻错误，MAE 也由 2.044 降至 0.101
%（Table~\ref{tab:rationale-correction}）。这些干预结果进一步表明，
% 在所选有害案例中，RF 推理错误会影响后续评分。

\textbf{Score recovery after RF rationale correction provides
intervention evidence that reasoning errors affect final scoring.}
FixOrig reproduced the original NatRF error in 157 of 158 cases,
confirming the reliability of the fixed-prefix rescoring setup. With
this setup validated, FixCorr restored the gold score in 142 of 158
cases, while all remaining 16 cases improved from severe to adjacent
errors, reducing MAE from 2.044 to 0.101
(Table~\ref{tab:rationale-correction}). These intervention results
further show that, within the selected harmful cases, errors in RF
reasoning affect subsequent scoring.

% \paragraph{RQ3：PaIR 和 RePaIR 如何缓解这两个问题？}
\paragraph{RQ3: How Do PaIR and RePaIR Address These Problems?}

% Figure~\ref{fig:rq3-method-results} 比较了 ReaSon、PaIR 和 RePaIR
% 相对标准 RS 的变化。ReaSon 将 RF 序数 QWK 从 0.679 提高到
% 0.707.PaIR 主要改善 DS 性能，而
% RePaIR 在保持 DS 表现的同时取得最大的 RF 序数提升。完整的
% 逐任务结果见 Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}。

Figure~\ref{fig:rq3-method-results} compares ReaSon, PaIR, and RePaIR
against standard RS. ReaSon raises RF ordinal QWK from 0.679 to 0.707. 
PaIR primarily improves DS performance, whereas
RePaIR retains the DS gains while producing the largest improvement in
RF ordinal scoring. Full task-level results are provided in
Appendix~\ref{app:full-results}.

% \textbf{ReaSon 主要改善了 RF 分支的序数评分。}
% Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level} 显示，在 RF 下，ReaSon 将
% 四个序数任务的平均 QWK 从 0.679 提高到 0.707，且每个任务均
% 有所提升，而二分类任务的 Macro-F1 基本不变（0.922 对 0.921）。
% ReaSon 的增益主要集中在 RF 序数评分；由于训练中未包含 DS prompt，
% 其 DS 输出存在明显的格式问题
%（Table~\ref{tab:strict-invalid-rates-all-methods}）。

\textbf{ReaSon primarily improves ordinal scoring on the RF branch.}
Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level} shows that, under RF, ReaSon
raises the mean QWK across the four ordinal tasks from 0.679 to 0.707,
with improvements on every task, while leaving binary Macro-F1 nearly
unchanged (0.922 versus 0.921). ReaSon's gains are therefore
concentrated in RF ordinal scoring; because DS prompts are absent during
training, its DS outputs exhibit substantial formatting failures
(Table~\ref{tab:strict-invalid-rates-all-methods}).

% \textbf{PaIR 主要改善了 DS 分支的评分性能。}
% Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level} 显示，在 DS 下，PaIR 相比
% 标准 RS 将四个序数任务的平均 QWK 从 0.696 提高到 0.757，
% 并将三个二分类任务的平均 Macro-F1 从 0.843 提高到 0.934，
% 且七个任务均有所提升。Table~\ref{tab:pair-mix-ablation} 中的
% Mix 消融实验进一步显示，在配对训练数据保持一致时，PaIR 在 DS
% 和 RF 下分别将 QWK 提高 0.015 和 0.008，并将 Macro-F1 提高
% 0.009 和 0.003。这一结果支持将 PaIR 的额外收益归因于分支级
% 损失归一化，而非配对数据本身。

\textbf{PaIR primarily improves scoring on the DS branch.}
Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level} shows that, under DS, PaIR
raises the mean QWK across the four ordinal tasks from 0.696 to 0.757
and the mean Macro-F1 across the three binary tasks from 0.843 to
0.934 relative to standard RS, with improvements on all seven tasks.
The Mix ablation in Table~\ref{tab:pair-mix-ablation} further shows
that, with the paired training data held fixed, PaIR improves QWK by
0.015 under DS and 0.008 under RF, and Macro-F1 by 0.009 and 0.003,
respectively. These results support attributing PaIR's additional gains
to branch-wise loss normalization rather than to the paired data itself.

\textbf{RePaIR combines ReaSon and PaIR to improve scoring under RF
while preserving DS performance.}
Figure~\ref{fig:rq3-method-results} and
Table~\ref{tab:full-method-task-level} show that, under DS, RePaIR
achieves a QWK of 0.758 and a Macro-F1 of 0.931, closely matching PaIR
at 0.757 and 0.934. Under RF, RePaIR raises QWK from PaIR's 0.698 to
0.735, also exceeding ReaSon's 0.707, and achieves the best result on
all four ordinal tasks, while keeping Macro-F1 close to PaIR (0.926
versus 0.929). These results show that RePaIR combines the complementary
strengths of ReaSon and PaIR, preserving PaIR's DS performance while
further improving ReaSon's RF scoring.
