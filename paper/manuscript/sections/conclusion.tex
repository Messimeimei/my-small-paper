\section{Conclusion}

% 本文研究了监督目标与推理协议如何共同影响科学写作评价模型。在当前
% Qwen3-4B 任务设置和外部评价下，RS 虽然改善了教师 rationale 拟合和
% 生成质量，但这一优势并未转化为更好的评分性能。在序数任务中，RF
% 增加了严重错误，并在两种监督目标下降低了 QWK；修正错误 rationale
% 则使大部分所选有害案例恢复正确。在已评估配置中，RePaIR 的 DS 性能
% 接近 PaIR，并在两种模型规模下取得最高的 RF 平均序数 QWK。总体而言，
% 当前结果表明 rationale 质量不能单独代表评分能力，监督目标应与实际
% 使用的推理协议联合设计和评估。

This paper examined how supervision objectives and inference protocols
jointly affect LLM-based scientific-writing evaluation. In our Qwen3-4B task
setting and external assessments, RS improved teacher-rationale fitting and
generated-rationale quality without improving scoring performance. On ordinal
tasks, RF increased severe errors and reduced QWK under both supervision
objectives, whereas correcting erroneous rationales recovered most selected
harmful cases. Among the evaluated configurations, RePaIR retains DS performance
close to PaIR and achieves the highest mean RF ordinal QWK at both model sizes.
These findings indicate that rationale quality alone is not a reliable proxy
for scoring ability, and that supervision objectives should be designed and
evaluated together with the intended inference protocol.

% \paragraph{局限性。}
\section{Limitations}

% 本研究的受控微调实验仅在 Qwen3-0.6B、Qwen3-4B 和七个科学写作评价任务上
% 采用固定 LoRA 配置进行。此外，rationale 质量分析依赖外部 LLM 裁判，修正实验
% 也仅覆盖预先筛选的有害案例。因此，当前结果不能直接推广至其他
% 模型和任务，也不能证明生成的 rationale 忠实反映模型的内部推理。
% 未来工作应在更多模型、任务和完整样本上进行验证，并引入人工评价。

Our controlled fine-tuning experiments are limited to two Qwen3 model
sizes, a fixed LoRA configuration, and seven scientific-writing evaluation
tasks. Moreover,
rationale quality was assessed
by external LLM judges, and the correction experiment covered only selected
harmful cases. The findings may therefore not generalize to other models or
tasks, nor do they establish that generated rationales faithfully represent
internal reasoning. Future work should extend the evaluation to broader models,
tasks, and samples, with additional human assessment.
