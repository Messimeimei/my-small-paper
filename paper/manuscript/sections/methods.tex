We first use a $2\times2$ controlled design to separate the
training-time supervision target from the test-time inference protocol.
The supervision factor contrasts score-only supervision (SO) with
rationale-and-score supervision (RS), while the inference factor
contrasts direct-score inference (DS) with rationale-first inference
(RF). We then introduce two targeted interventions. ReaSon changes the
source of rationale supervision, whereas PaIR improves interface
coverage through paired DS and RF training views with branch-wise
loss normalization. Because these interventions target distinct
aspects of RS, RePaIR applies the PaIR objective to regenerated-rationale
training data to test whether their effects are complementary.
Mix serves as a matched ablation for PaIR.

\subsection{Controlled Supervision--Inference Design}

% 设 \(x_i\) 为第 \(i\) 个样本的输入，\(s_i\) 为 gold score，
% \(r_i^T\) 为教师模型生成的 rationale。SO 与 RS 的训练目标分别为
% \[
% \begin{aligned}
% y_i^{\mathrm{SO}} ={}&
% \texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
% \texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}, \\
% y_i^{\mathrm{RS}} ={}&
% \texttt{\textless reasoning\textgreater}\,r_i^T\,
% \texttt{\textless/reasoning\textgreater} \\
% &\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
% \texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}.
% \end{aligned}
% \]
% 两种训练形式共享输入、gold score 和数据划分，仅 system instruction
% 与目标 completion 不同。训练完成后，每个 SO 和 RS adapter 均在 DS 和 RF 两种协议下评估，
% 得到四种受控实验条件：SO\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\)、SO\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\)、
% RS\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\) 和 RS\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\)。其中，SO 和 RS 表示
% 训练时监督目标，DS 和 RF 表示测试时推理协议。

Let \(x_i\) denote the input for the \(i\)-th example, \(s_i\) its gold
score, and \(r_i^T\) the rationale generated by the teacher model. The
SO and RS target completions are, respectively,
\[
\begin{aligned}
y_i^{\mathrm{SO}} ={}&
\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
\texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}, \\
y_i^{\mathrm{RS}} ={}&
\texttt{\textless reasoning\textgreater}\,r_i^T\,
\texttt{\textless/reasoning\textgreater} \\
&\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
\texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The two training formats share the same inputs, gold scores, and data
splits; they differ only in their system instructions and target
completions. After training, each SO and RS adapter is evaluated under both DS and
RF, yielding four controlled conditions: SO\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\),
SO\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\), RS\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\), and
RS\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\). Here, SO and RS denote the training-time
supervision targets, whereas DS and RF denote the test-time inference
protocols.

% 下面进一步形式化标准 RS 的损失。标准 RS 对整个 completion 中的
% token loss 统一取平均。因此，其训练目标可以分解为
% \[
% \begin{aligned}
% \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RS}}
% ={}&
% \frac{N_r}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_r \\
% &+
% \frac{N_s}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_s ,
% \end{aligned}
% \]
% 其中，\(N_r\) 和 \(N_s\) 分别表示 rationale 和 score 部分
% 的 token 数量，\(\mathcal{L}_r\) 和 \(\mathcal{L}_s\)
% 表示对应的平均 token-level loss。当 \(N_r \gg N_s\) 时，
% rationale 部分在总体标量损失中具有更大的权重。

Standard RS averages token losses over the entire completion.
Its objective can therefore be decomposed as
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RS}}
={}&
\frac{N_r}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_r
&+
\frac{N_s}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_s ,
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(N_r\) and \(N_s\) are the numbers of rationale and score
tokens, and \(\mathcal{L}_r\) and \(\mathcal{L}_s\) are their
respective mean token-level losses. When \(N_r \gg N_s\), the
rationale span receives a larger weight in the overall scalar loss.

% \paragraph{标准 RS 的局限。}
% 标准 RS 在训练与 RF 推理之间存在潜在的 rationale 来源失配。
% 训练时，score token 以教师生成的 rationale \(r_i^T\) 为条件进行学习；
% 而在 RF 推理时，模型首先生成自身的 rationale \(\hat{r}_i\)，再基于
% 该 rationale 输出分数。教师 rationale 与模型自生成 rationale
% 在内容和错误模式上可能存在差异，因此，score token 在训练与推理
% 阶段所依赖的 rationale 上下文并不完全一致。此外，标准 RS 对整个
% completion 的 token loss 进行平均，使较长的 rationale span 相比
% 简短的 score span 对总体损失具有更大的贡献。标准 RS 微调还仅使用
% RF 格式的训练样本，因此模型在训练过程中没有接触 DS 接口。

\paragraph{Limitations of Standard RS.}
Standard RS is subject to a potential rationale-source mismatch between
training and RF inference. During training, the score tokens are learned
conditional on the teacher-generated rationale \(r_i^T\). Under RF
inference, the model instead generates its own rationale \(\hat{r}_i\)
before producing the score. Teacher and model-generated rationales may
differ in their content and error patterns, so the score tokens depend
on different rationale contexts during training and inference. Standard
RS also averages token losses over the full completion, giving the
longer rationale span a greater contribution to the overall loss than
the short score span. Moreover, standard RS fine-tuning uses only
RF-formatted examples, leaving the DS interface unseen during training.

\subsection{ReaSon: Regenerated Rationales with Gold-Score Restoration}

Under standard RS, teacher rationales \(r_i^T\) are paired with gold
scores \(s_i\) to form
\(\mathcal{D}_T=\{(x_i,r_i^T,s_i)\}\). A model is initialized from the
base model \(\theta_0\) and trained on this data to obtain the
first-stage model \(\theta_A\). ReaSon then uses \(\theta_A\) to
regenerate a rationale \(r_i^A\) and a predicted score
\(\hat{s}_i^A\) for each training example under RF. Data reconstruction
retains \(r_i^A\) and replaces \(\hat{s}_i^A\) with the original gold
score \(s_i\), yielding
\(\mathcal{D}_A=\{(x_i,r_i^A,s_i)\}\). The complete procedure can be summarized as
\[
\mathcal{D}_T
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{RS}}
\theta_A
\xrightarrow[\hat{s}_i^A\mapsto s_i]{\mathrm{RF}}
\mathcal{D}_A
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{RS}}
\theta_B .
\]
The final model \(\theta_B\) is reinitialized from the base model
\(\theta_0\) and trained on \(\mathcal{D}_A\), without inheriting the
parameters of \(\theta_A\). ReaSon keeps the base-model initialization, RS
objective, and gold-score supervision fixed, changing only the source
of rationale supervision used to train the final model~\citep{chiang-etal-2025-tract}. The construction of the ReaSon training data is described in
Appendix~\ref{tab:constructing-reason-data}, while the model and decoding configuration
for teacher-rationale distillation are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:teacher-config}, with the corresponding prompts shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:rs}.

\subsection{Paired-Interface Reweighting (PaIR)}

Standard RS trains only on RF completions, so rationale tokens receive
most of the token-averaged loss weight and the DS interface remains
unsupervised. PaIR constructs paired DS and RF training views for each
example. The two views share the same user prompt and gold score but
use different system prompts and output structures: the DS target
\(y_i^D\) contains only the score block, whereas the RF target
\(y_i^R\) contains a rationale block followed by the same score block.

To prevent the longer RF view from receiving greater weight solely
because it contains more tokens, PaIR computes the mean token-level
losses \(\mathcal{L}_D\) and \(\mathcal{L}_R\) separately and combines
them equally~\citep{shi-etal-2025-investigating}:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PaIR}}
=
0.5\mathcal{L}_D
+
0.5\mathcal{L}_R.
\]
The resulting scalar loss is used for a single backward pass and
parameter update. Equal weighting is applied between the DS and RF
views; all supervised tokens within the RF view remain averaged under
a shared denominator.

We construct two ablations, PaIR-BP and PaIR-SV, to evaluate equal
branch weighting and paired-view supervision, respectively. PaIR-BP
(PaIR with Branch Pooling) retains the paired DS and RF views but
averages all supervised tokens from both views under a shared
denominator. Let \(M_D\) and \(M_R\) denote the numbers of supervised
tokens in the DS and RF views, respectively. Its objective is
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PaIR\text{-}BP}}
=
\frac{
M_D\mathcal{L}_D
+
M_R\mathcal{L}_R
}{
M_D+M_R
}.
\]

PaIR-SV (PaIR with a Single RF View) retains only one RF training view
and separately averages the token losses over its rationale and score
regions. During training, the score branch attends only to the prompt
and preceding score tokens, not to rationale tokens. Let
\(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RF,rat}}\) and
\(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RF,score}}\) denote the mean losses over the
rationale and score regions of the same RF view, respectively. Its
objective is
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PaIR\text{-}SV}}
=
0.5\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RF,rat}}
+
0.5\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RF,score}}.
\]
The PaIR-BP settings and results are reported in the third subsection
of Appendix~\ref{app:dataset}, Table~\ref{tab:train-infer-configs},
and Table~\ref{tab:pair-mix-ablation}; the PaIR-SV settings and
format-validity results are reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:ssa-v2-ablation} and
Table~\ref{tab:ssa-v2-format-validity}.

\subsection{Regenerated-Rationale Paired-Interface Reweighting (RePaIR)}

Although PaIR provides paired DS/RF supervision and balances the two
branch losses, its RF view still relies on teacher rationales; RePaIR
therefore regenerates the RF rationales with a first-stage PaIR model
while retaining the paired-view objective. The first-stage model
\(\theta_A\) is initialized from the base model \(\theta_0\) and
trained with PaIR using paired DS/RF views constructed from the
teacher-rationale data \(\mathcal{D}_T\). Under RF, \(\theta_A\) then
generates a new rationale \(r_i^A\) and predicted score
\(\hat{s}_i^A\). Data reconstruction retains \(r_i^A\) and replaces
the predicted score with the gold score \(s_i\), yielding
\(\mathcal{D}_A=\{(x_i,r_i^A,s_i)\}\). The final model \(\theta_B\)
is reinitialized from \(\theta_0\) and trained with PaIR using new
paired DS/RF views constructed from \(\mathcal{D}_A\). The complete
procedure is
\[
\mathcal{D}_T
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{PaIR}}
\theta_A
\xrightarrow[\hat{s}_i^A\mapsto s_i]{\mathrm{RF}}
\mathcal{D}_A
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{PaIR}}
\theta_B .
\]
RePaIR data construction is described in the second subsection of
Appendix~\ref{tab:constructing-pair-data}; the training configuration and DS/RF
system prompts are reported in Table~\ref{tab:train-infer-configs}
and Figures~\ref{fig:so} and~\ref{fig:rs}, respectively.
