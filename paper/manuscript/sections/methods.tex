% 随后，我们适配两种已有训练策略，用于考察 rationale 再生成和

We first use a $2\times2$ controlled design to separate the
training-time supervision target from the test-time inference protocol.
The supervision factor contrasts score-only supervision (SO) with
rationale-and-score supervision (RS), while the inference factor
contrasts direct-score inference (DS) with rationale-first inference
(RF). We then adapt two established training strategies to examine
rationale regeneration and supervision of both inference interfaces.
ReaSon instantiates a two-stage regeneration strategy, whereas PaIR
adapts separate-loss training to paired DS/RF views. RePaIR combines
the two strategies, and we evaluate the resulting configuration under
both interfaces.
PaIR-BP serves as a matched ablation for PaIR.

\subsection{Controlled Supervision--Inference Design}

% 设 \(x_i\) 为第 \(i\) 个样本的输入，\(s_i\) 为 gold score，
% \(r_i^T\) 为教师模型生成的 rationale。SO 与 RS 的训练目标分别为
% \[
% \begin{aligned}
% y_i^{\mathrm{SO}} ={}&
% \texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
% \texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}, \\
% y_i^{\mathrm{RS}} ={}&
% \texttt{\textless reasoning\textgreater}\,r_i^T\,
% \texttt{\textless/reasoning\textgreater} \\
% &\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
% \texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}.
% \end{aligned}
% \]
% 两种训练形式共享输入、gold score 和数据划分，仅 system instruction
% 与目标 completion 不同。训练完成后，每个 SO 和 RS adapter 均在 DS 和 RF 两种协议下评估，
% 得到四种受控实验条件：SO\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\)、SO\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\)、
% RS\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\) 和 RS\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\)。其中，SO 和 RS 表示
% 训练时监督目标，DS 和 RF 表示测试时推理协议。

Let \(x_i\) denote the input for the \(i\)-th example, \(s_i\) its gold
score, and \(r_i^T\) the rationale generated by the teacher model. The
SO and RS target completions are, respectively,
\[
\begin{aligned}
y_i^{\mathrm{SO}} ={}&
\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
\texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}, \\
y_i^{\mathrm{RS}} ={}&
\texttt{\textless reasoning\textgreater}\,r_i^T\,
\texttt{\textless/reasoning\textgreater} \\
&\texttt{\textless score\textgreater}\,s_i\,
\texttt{\textless/score\textgreater}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The two training formats share the same inputs, gold scores, and data
splits; they differ only in their system instructions and target
completions. After training, each SO and RS adapter is evaluated under both DS and
RF, yielding four controlled conditions: SO\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\),
SO\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\), RS\(_{\mathrm{DS}}\), and
RS\(_{\mathrm{RF}}\). Here, SO and RS denote the training-time
supervision targets, whereas DS and RF denote the test-time inference
protocols.

% 下面进一步形式化标准 RS 的损失。标准 RS 对整个 completion 中的
% token loss 统一取平均。因此，其训练目标可以分解为
% \[
% \begin{aligned}
% \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RS}}
% ={}&
% \frac{N_r}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_r \\
% &+
% \frac{N_s}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_s ,
% \end{aligned}
% \]
% 其中，\(N_r\) 和 \(N_s\) 分别表示 rationale 和 score 部分
% 的 token 数量，\(\mathcal{L}_r\) 和 \(\mathcal{L}_s\)
% 表示对应的平均 token-level loss。当 \(N_r \gg N_s\) 时，
% rationale 部分在总体标量损失中具有更大的权重。

Standard RS averages token losses over the entire completion.
Its objective can therefore be decomposed as
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RS}}
={}&
\frac{N_r}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_r
&+
\frac{N_s}{N_r+N_s}\mathcal{L}_s ,
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(N_r\) and \(N_s\) are the numbers of rationale and score
tokens, and \(\mathcal{L}_r\) and \(\mathcal{L}_s\) are their
respective mean token-level losses. When \(N_r \gg N_s\), the
rationale span receives a larger weight in the overall scalar loss.

% \paragraph{标准 RS 的局限。}
% 标准 RS 存在三个潜在局限：训练时的 score 以教师 rationale 为条件，
% 而 RF 推理依赖模型自生成 rationale；对完整 completion 的 token loss
% 取平均会使较长的 rationale span 获得更大权重；仅使用 RF 格式训练
% 则使 DS 接口在训练中不可见。

\paragraph{Limitations of Standard RS.}
Standard RS has three potential limitations. The score is conditioned on
teacher rationales during training but on model-generated rationales under
RF inference. Averaging token losses over the full completion gives the
longer rationale span greater weight than the short score span. Training
only on RF-formatted examples also leaves the DS interface unseen.

\subsection{ReaSon: Regenerated Rationales with Gold-Score Restoration}

ReaSon follows the two-stage rationale-regeneration strategy studied by
\citet{chiang-etal-2025-tract}. We use it as a CE-based comparison
configuration to examine regeneration under our scientific-writing tasks
and DS/RF evaluation protocol.
Under standard RS, teacher rationales \(r_i^T\) are paired with gold
scores \(s_i\) to form
\(\mathcal{D}_T=\{(x_i,r_i^T,s_i)\}\). A model is initialized from the
base model \(\theta_0\) and trained on this data to obtain the
first-stage model \(\theta_A\). ReaSon then uses \(\theta_A\) to
regenerate a rationale \(r_i^A\) and a predicted score
\(\hat{s}_i^A\) for each training example under RF. Data reconstruction
retains \(r_i^A\) and replaces \(\hat{s}_i^A\) with the original gold
score \(s_i\), yielding
\(\mathcal{D}_A=\{(x_i,r_i^A,s_i)\}\). The complete procedure can be summarized as
\[
\mathcal{D}_T
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{RS}}
\theta_A
\xrightarrow[\hat{s}_i^A\mapsto s_i]{\mathrm{RF}}
\mathcal{D}_A
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{RS}}
\theta_B .
\]
The final model \(\theta_B\) is reinitialized from the base model
\(\theta_0\) and trained on \(\mathcal{D}_A\), without inheriting the
parameters of \(\theta_A\). For the final-stage comparison with RS,
ReaSon retains the base-model initialization, CE objective, gold scores,
and training schedule while replacing teacher rationales with first-stage
model outputs. The construction of the ReaSon training data is described in
Appendix~\ref{app:reason-training-data}, while the model and decoding configuration
for teacher-rationale distillation are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:teacher-config}, with the corresponding prompts shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:rs}.

\subsection{Paired-Interface Reweighting (PaIR)}

PaIR adapts the separate-loss training principle of
Align~\citep{shi-etal-2025-investigating} to explicitly formatted DS and RF
views. Each example produces two training sequences with the same user
input and gold score: a DS completion containing only the score, and an
RF completion containing a rationale followed by the score. The two
sequences use interface-specific system prompts.

PaIR computes the mean cross-entropy over supervised tokens
separately for each view, yielding \(\mathcal{L}_D\) and
\(\mathcal{L}_R\), and combines them equally:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PaIR}}
=
0.5\mathcal{L}_D
+
0.5\mathcal{L}_R.
\]
Here, each loss is averaged over the supervised tokens of its corresponding
view. The RF loss includes both rationale and score tokens. Equal view
weighting prevents the longer RF completion from dominating solely through
its token count.

We examine two comparison variants.
PaIR-BP retains both views but pools all supervised-token losses
into a single mean, testing the effect of equal view weighting.
PaIR-SV explores a single RF view with separately normalized
rationale and score losses, while preventing score tokens
from attending to rationale tokens.
Full definitions, settings, and results for PaIR-BP appear in
Appendices~\ref{tab:constructing-pair-data} and
\ref{app:experimental-details}, and Table~\ref{tab:pair-bp-ablation};
definitions, settings, and
format-validity results for PaIR-SV appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:ssa-v2-ablation} and
Table~\ref{tab:ssa-v2-format-validity}.

\subsection{Regenerated-Rationale Paired-Interface Reweighting (RePaIR)}

RePaIR combines the paired DS/RF objective with rationale regeneration.
A first-stage PaIR model generates replacement rationales, and a fresh
base model is trained on the resulting paired views using the same
objective. We evaluate whether this combined configuration improves RF
scoring while maintaining DS performance. The first-stage model
\(\theta_A\) is initialized from the base model \(\theta_0\) and
trained with PaIR using paired DS/RF views constructed from the
teacher-rationale data \(\mathcal{D}_T\). Under RF, \(\theta_A\) then
generates a new rationale \(r_i^A\) and predicted score
\(\hat{s}_i^A\). Data reconstruction retains \(r_i^A\) and replaces
the predicted score with the gold score \(s_i\), yielding
\(\mathcal{D}_A=\{(x_i,r_i^A,s_i)\}\). The final model \(\theta_B\)
is reinitialized from \(\theta_0\) and trained with PaIR using new
paired DS/RF views constructed from \(\mathcal{D}_A\). The complete
procedure is
\[
\mathcal{D}_T
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{PaIR}}
\theta_A
\xrightarrow[\hat{s}_i^A\mapsto s_i]{\mathrm{RF}}
\mathcal{D}_A
\xrightarrow[\theta_0]{\mathrm{PaIR}}
\theta_B .
\]
Regenerated-rationale data construction is described in
Appendix~\ref{app:reason-training-data}, and paired DS/RF view
construction is detailed in Appendix~\ref{tab:constructing-pair-data}.
The training configuration is provided in
Appendix~\ref{app:experimental-details}, and the DS/RF system prompts are
shown in the corresponding panels of Figure~\ref{fig:so}.
